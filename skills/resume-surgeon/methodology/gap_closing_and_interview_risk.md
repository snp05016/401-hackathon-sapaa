# Evidence Gaps and Interview Risk

Resume tailoring should improve how authentic experience is presented, not manufacture missing qualifications.

## Gap classification

For each job requirement, classify the candidate's evidence as:

- `direct`: demonstrated in the same context with a concrete example;
- `related`: adjacent experience with a defensible transfer;
- `coursework`: academic or training exposure;
- `familiarity`: explicitly confirmed but without a substantial example;
- `unknown`: no supporting evidence;
- `missing`: the candidate explicitly says they do not have it.

Only direct, related, coursework, or confirmed familiarity may appear on the resume, and the wording must reflect the actual depth. Unknown and missing items remain in the gap report.

## Focused elicitation

Ask at most three questions in one round. Prioritize high-value requirements that could change the tailoring result. Questions should name the requirement and ask for a concrete example, source, scope, or outcome without suggesting the desired answer.

Good:

> The role asks for PostgreSQL performance work. Did you design indexes, analyze query plans, or tune connection pooling in any listed role or project? If so, which one and what evidence can support it?

Bad:

> Can we say you optimized PostgreSQL in your last project?

## Interview-risk audit

Challenge each changed bullet with:

1. What exactly did the candidate do?
2. What evidence ties the claim to this role or project?
3. Is the ownership level accurate?
4. Can the metric be explained and sourced?
5. Does the wording imply production depth beyond the evidence?
6. Could the candidate defend the claim in a detailed interview?

If a claim fails, weaken it to the supported level, restore the original, or remove the proposed edit.

## Gap output

For every unresolved requirement, report:

- canonical skill or qualification;
- required versus preferred status;
- current evidence level;
- whether it was intentionally omitted from the resume;
- an optional learning or portfolio suggestion, clearly separate from the tailored resume.
